Historical development

Revenue increased from £18.0m to £25.0m between FY2023 and FY2025 (17.9% CAGR). Management EBITDA rose from £2.7m to £5.0m. Reported margins were 15.0%, 18.1% and 20.0% across the three years.

Diligence focus

The earnings basis is management-reported. QoE, normalisation, debt, cash and working-capital schedules are unavailable. The revenue difference requires reconciliation before relying on the growth trend.
