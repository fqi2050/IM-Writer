Revenue characteristics

Maintenance accounts for 70% of revenue. Agreements generally last one to three years, with renewal by agreement and price reviews subject to terms and customer acceptance. In the FY2025 due-for-renewal cohort, 23 of 25 agreements renewed. Retained annual value was £3.72m on a £4.00m starting base, before price or scope changes.

Ownership and sale scope

Founder ownership is 70%, management 20% and the seed investor 10%. The proposed sale is 70%–100% of the standalone UK company, including its hubs, operating assets and customer contracts. The process is at preparation stage. No valuation, rollover, financing structure or timetable has been agreed. Contract consents and legal title require review. The FY2027 forecast depends on contract wins and hiring. Pipeline remains unsigned.

Reporting basis and unresolved revenue difference

The CFO supports provisional use of the £25.0m management model. The earlier briefing reports £26.2m, a £1.2m difference. A possible £0.8m order-timing item has not been reconciled to invoices or the ledger. Quarterly figures agree to the annual model. Cash and bank debt drafts are available. No QoE or verified add-backs support normalised earnings.

Arithmetic explanation of EBITDA growth
