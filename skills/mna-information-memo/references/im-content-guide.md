# IM content guide

Use the client's investment case and transaction perimeter. A ten-page example can cover: cover, executive summary, investment highlights, business and services, customers, operations and management, historical financials, growth and outlook, transaction overview, key considerations. This is an example sequence, not a mandatory ten-page limit for other projects.

Investment highlights need evidence and an appropriate qualifier. A contracted revenue share is not ARR, guaranteed renewal or revenue retention. Management-reported EBITDA is not adjusted or audited EBITDA. Proposed rollover is not agreed ownership. A growth forecast is not signed backlog. Never infer a market-share claim from company size.

Financial pages preserve periods, scale and basis. Show annual revenue, cost of services, gross profit, operating expenses, EBITDA and margin where available. Show forecasts separately or clearly label each period. CAGR uses the number of year intervals, and margin uses the same period/perimeter revenue. Cash conversion, debt-free/cash-free pricing and normalised working capital require their own sources.

Use investment claims that are informative without erasing uncertainty. Keep material source conflicts on the relevant page. Put detailed tracing in speaker notes. Avoid adding generic market pages with unsourced statistics merely to complete a template.

Choose a neutral unbranded style until a client or firm template is supplied. Existing files in the workspace are not automatically authorised design references. A subsequent template-matching task should inspect the selected reference explicitly.
